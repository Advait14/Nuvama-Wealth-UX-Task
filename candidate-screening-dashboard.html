How Role Alignment Works

1. Evidence-based scoring: Self-ratings are displayed but never verify a capability on their own. Verified criteria require linked project samples, stated team contributions, or verified tools.

2. Gate criteria: Eligibility (citizenship/visa) and baseline seniority serve as strict gates. If an applicant has a visa blocker, they are flagged regardless of skill scores.

3. Reviewer oversight: The alignment badge serves as a starting point. Recruiters can click Override inside any profile to change the evaluation based on qualitative interview findings.
